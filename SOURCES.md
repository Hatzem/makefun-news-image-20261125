6. **Landmine casualties hit a five-year high** — Landmines and explosive remnants
   of war killed or injured at least 6,929 people in 2025, the highest yearly toll

   released in Geneva on Tuesday. Civilians made up 84% of casualties, and where
   the age was known, children accounted for half of civilian casualties. Myanmar
   recorded the most casualties for the third year running. The report was released
   on Tuesday 24 November and reported worldwide on Wednesday 25 November.
